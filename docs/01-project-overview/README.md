## 🎯 Key Information

- **Project Name**: Medical Expert System for Malaria and Typhoid Fever (MESMTF)
- **Client**: Ministry of Health and Social Services
- **Competition Period**: 19-28 September 2025
- **System Type**: Web-based Medical Expert System

## 🏥 System Overview

MESMTF is designed to provide comprehensive e-Health services including:
- Medical records management
- Appointment scheduling
- AI-powered diagnosis
- Treatment planning
- Pharmacy management
- Drug administration
- Comprehensive reporting
